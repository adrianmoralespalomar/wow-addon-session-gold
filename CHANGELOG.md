# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Session balance in the bags, next to the gold (game's bags and Baganator).
- Income and expenses by source and gold per hour when hovering over your gold, plus today's balance and the last session's.
- The session survives `/reload`; the last session summary is printed when logging in.
- Options with `/sgold` and in *Options → AddOns*. English and Spanish.
